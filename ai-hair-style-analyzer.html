The 90s Blowout Hair: Why It’s Taking Over 2026

If you've spent any time on Pinterest or TikTok lately, you've likely noticed a massive resurgence of the 90s Blowout Hair trend. Moving away from the completely sleek, flat-ironed looks of the 2010s, this beauty movement is all about embracing maximum volume, bouncy layers, and effortless glamour.

What is a 90s Blowout? Characterized by dramatic, swooping layers, extreme root volume, and ends that flip inward or outward, the 90s blowout mimics the iconic looks of supermodels from three decades ago. Achieved using a round brush, hot rollers, or modern air-wrap styling tools, it’s a style that breathes life and movement into the hair.

Why is it trending? In 2026, the beauty paradigm has shifted towards "high-maintenance to be low-maintenance." A fantastic blowout can last for days, looking slightly more lived-in and beautiful as the week progresses. Furthermore, it adds a touch of accessible luxury to any aesthetic, elevating simple outfits with a crown of healthy, vibrant hair.

Best Face Shapes for this Trend:

  • Round Faces: The vertical volume at the crown elongates the face perfectly.
  • Square Faces: The soft, sweeping curves of the blowout soften strong, angular jawlines beautifully.
  • Oval Faces: As the universal face shape, an oval structure carries the volume of a 90s blowout without being overwhelmed.

Expert Styling Tips: Always start with a volumizing mousse on damp hair. Focus on over-directing the roots upward while drying, and use velcro rollers while the hair is still hot to set the volume. Lock it in with a flexible hold hairspray to maintain that bouncy movement.

Best Hairstyle For Face Shape: The Breakdown

Your face shape is the foundational architectural blueprint for your overall aesthetic. Utilizing a Face Shape Analyzer isn't just about following rules; it's about understanding geometry to highlight your best features. Here is our expert breakdown of haircut recommendations based on your specific profile:

1. The Oval Face Shape

Often considered the most versatile canvas, oval faces possess balanced proportions—slightly wider at the cheekbones and tapering gently toward the chin. Because the proportions are already perfectly symmetrical, you can pull off nearly any style. Recommendation: A blunt bob or long, beachy waves. Avoid styles that cover up your face too much; let your natural symmetry shine.

2. The Round Face Shape

Round faces feature soft curves with the width and length being roughly equal. The goal here is to create the illusion of length and angles. Recommendation: Long layers that begin below the chin, or an asymmetrical lob (long bob). Avoid blunt bangs or tight, voluminous curls right at the cheeks, which can add unwanted width.

3. The Square Face Shape

Defined by a strong, prominent jawline and a broad forehead, square faces exude power and structure. The styling objective is to soften those sharp angles. Recommendation: Wispy, curtain bangs, soft romantic layers, or a side-swept parting. Textured, wavy hair works wonders to diffuse the sharpness of the jawline.

4. The Heart Face Shape

Heart-shaped faces are wider at the forehead and gently narrow down to a delicate, pointed chin. Recommendation: Bobs and lobs that end right at the jawline are incredibly flattering, as they add volume exactly where the face is narrowest. Deep side parts and sweeping bangs also help minimize forehead width.

5. The Diamond Face Shape

Characterized by high, striking cheekbones with a narrow forehead and jawline. Recommendation: Face-framing layers that hit around the chin to add width to the lower face. A sleek ponytail that pulls hair back tightly will beautifully showcase your phenomenal cheekbones.

6. The Oblong (Long) Face Shape

Oblong faces are similar to oval but noticeably longer and slightly narrower. The goal is to add width rather than length. Recommendation: Classic blunt bangs are a game-changer here, as they visually shorten the face. Shoulder-length cuts with plenty of lateral volume (like the 90s blowout) are ideal.

Hair Color Recommendations: Finding Your Tone

Our AI Hair Style Analyzer doesn't just look at geometry; it factors in the crucial element of color theory. Choosing the right hair color can drastically improve your complexion, making your skin look brighter and your eyes pop. It all comes down to your skin's undertones.

Warm Tones: If your skin has yellow, peachy, or golden undertones (veins appear greenish), you thrive in warm, rich colors. Recommendations: Honey blonde, copper red, rich caramel, and golden chestnut. These shades will make your skin glow and look inherently sun-kissed.

Cool Tones: If your skin has pink, red, or bluish undertones (veins appear blue/purple), you need colors that offer contrast without clashing. Recommendations: Icy platinum blonde, ash brown, cool raven black, or even trendy silver and pastel tones. These shades neutralize redness in the skin.

Neutral Tones: If you can't quite tell if your veins are green or blue, you likely have neutral undertones. You hit the genetic lottery in color theory. Recommendations: You can pull off almost anything, but muted, multi-dimensional colors like "bronde" (brown-blonde), soft mocha, and strawberry blonde look exceptionally natural and luxurious.

Trending Pinterest Colors for 2026: We are seeing a massive surge in "expensive brunettes" (high-shine, deeply conditioned brown with subtle micro-highlights) and "cherry cola red" (a deep, vivid burgundy that works beautifully across many skin tones).
