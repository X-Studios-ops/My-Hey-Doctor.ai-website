☀️ Heat Stroke Symptoms, Causes & Prevention

Heat stroke is a serious medical condition that happens when the body temperature rises too high and cannot cool itself properly.

🚨 Common Heat Stroke Symptoms

  • High body temperature
  • Severe headache
  • Dizziness
  • Confusion
  • Rapid heartbeat
  • Nausea or vomiting
  • Hot and dry skin
  • Fainting

🔥 Causes of Heat Stroke

  • Extreme hot weather
  • Dehydration
  • Excessive physical activity
  • Staying in direct sunlight for long periods
  • Poor ventilation

💧 Prevention Tips

  • Drink enough water daily
  • Avoid outdoor activity during peak heat
  • Wear light-colored clothing
  • Stay in cool and shaded places
  • Use fans or air conditioning when possible

🏥 When to Seek Medical Help

Seek immediate medical attention if a person becomes unconscious, confused, develops a very high temperature, or has difficulty breathing.

❓ FAQ

Can heat stroke be dangerous?

Yes. Heat stroke can become a medical emergency if not treated quickly.

How much water should I drink in summer?

Water needs vary by age, activity level, and climate, but staying hydrated is important during hot weather.

What is the difference between heat exhaustion and heat stroke?

Heat stroke is generally more serious and may involve dangerously high body temperature and confusion.
