Protein Myths Busted

Let's separate fact from fiction regarding protein consumption.

Myth: High protein damages your kidneys.

For healthy individuals without pre-existing kidney disease, high protein diets are perfectly safe and do not harm renal function.

Fact: Kidneys are efficient filters designed to handle protein metabolism.

Myth: You can only absorb 30g of protein per meal.

The body can absorb much more than 30g at once. While muscle protein synthesis caps around 25-40g per meal, excess protein still provides calories and prevents muscle breakdown.

Fact: Total daily intake matters more than meal frequency.
