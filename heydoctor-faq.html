Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about how the Heydoctor.ai platform works, data privacy, and medical capabilities.

What is Heydoctor.ai?
Heydoctor.ai is an advanced multimodal AI health concierge engineered to provide instant, precision-driven wellness insights, preliminary symptom analysis, and interactive health tools like BMI calculators and hydration trackers.
Can Heydoctor.ai replace a real human doctor?
No. Heydoctor.ai is an informational health assistant designed to guide and educate users. It does not provide certified medical diagnoses or replace professional medical consultation. In emergencies, always dial your local emergency number (like 102 or 112).
How does the Neural Diagnostic Stream work?
Our Neural Diagnostic Stream uses advanced AI models to analyze the symptoms and health queries you provide. It matches them against vast medical knowledge to offer probable insights, home care suggestions, and general wellness tips instantly.
Is my health data and chat private?
Yes, privacy is our top priority. The AI processes your queries in real-time to generate responses, and we do not store personal medical records or chat histories for external tracking or profiling.
Who built Heydoctor.ai?
Heydoctor.ai is engineered and developed by Pratyush, focusing on autonomous medical and health companion systems for accessible digital wellness.
