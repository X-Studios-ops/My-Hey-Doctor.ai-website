Frequently Asked Questions

How does the AI Nutrition Planner work?

It uses your personal metrics like age, weight, and goals to calculate your BMR and TDEE, then tailors a meal plan to your specific health conditions.

Is this diet plan good for weight loss?

Yes, if you select 'Weight Loss' as your goal, the planner automatically generates a safe caloric deficit and recommends high-satiety foods.

Can it help with a Diabetes diet?

Absolutely. Selecting Diabetes will adjust the recommendations to include low-GI foods, lean proteins, and minimize refined sugars to prevent insulin spikes.

Are PCOS diets supported?

Yes, selecting PCOS optimizes the plan for hormone balance, focusing on high protein, low glycemic index carbs, and anti-inflammatory foods like flax seeds.

How accurate is the calorie planning?

It uses the clinically validated Mifflin-St Jeor equation to estimate your basal metabolic rate (BMR) and adjusts it based on your selected daily activity multiplier.

What is healthy eating?

Healthy eating involves a balanced intake of macronutrients (proteins, carbs, fats) and micronutrients (vitamins, minerals), tailored to your body's specific demands.

Can I save my diet plan?

Yes! You can download your plan as a PDF, print it directly, or save it locally to your device browser using the built-in premium action buttons.

Is this tool completely free?

Yes, this AI Nutrition Planner is 100% free. There are no external dependencies, no subscriptions, and no hidden paywalls protecting your health data.
