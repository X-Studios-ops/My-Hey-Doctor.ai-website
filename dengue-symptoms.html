Dengue Symptoms, Prevention & Treatment Guide

Dengue is a mosquito-borne viral disease that can cause high fever, headache, body pain and weakness. Early identification of symptoms is important.

Common Dengue Symptoms

  • High fever
  • Severe headache
  • Pain behind the eyes
  • Muscle and joint pain
  • Nausea and vomiting
  • Skin rash
  • Extreme fatigue

How to Prevent Dengue

  • Remove stagnant water
  • Use mosquito repellent
  • Wear full-sleeved clothes
  • Keep water containers covered
  • Keep surroundings clean

When To Seek Medical Help

If fever persists, symptoms worsen, or dehydration occurs, seek professional medical care immediately.
