🧘 Structural Master Library: Recommended Yoga Poses

Incorporate these essential poses into your practice. Ensure focus on stable, continuous breathing.

1. Tadasana (Mountain Pose)

Difficulty: Beginner Duration: 1-2 Mins

Benefits: Stabilizes posture, strengthens thighs and core, stabilizes daily balance patterns.
Precautions: Avoid holding breath if experiencing acute headaches or low blood pressure flareups.

2. Bhujangasana (Cobra Pose)

Difficulty: Beginner-Intermediate Duration: 30-60 Secs

Benefits: Opens up front lung tissue, tones deep abdominal muscles, improves lumbar flexibility.
Precautions: Avoid during pregnancy or if suffering from severe recent abdominal surgeries.

3. Vajrasana (Thunderbolt Pose)

Difficulty: Beginner Duration: 5-10 Mins post-meal

Benefits: Alters blood flow patterns to optimize digestive tract assimilation; strengthens ankles.
Precautions: Avoid if experiencing severe knee joint injuries or advanced osteoarthritis.

4. Surya Namaskar (Sun Salutations)

Difficulty: Intermediate Duration: 5-12 Rounds

Benefits: Superior cardiovascular conditioning, extensive dynamic flexibility enhancement, complete full-body engagement.
Precautions: Perform gently if you experience high blood pressure; modify transitions if wrists are weak.

5. Adho Mukha Svanasana (Downward-Facing Dog)

Difficulty: Beginner-Intermediate Duration: 1-2 Mins

Benefits: Lengthens the entire posterior chain (hamstrings, calves, spine); increases blood flow to the head and scalp.
Precautions: Modify using blocks if struggling with severe carpal tunnel syndrome.

6. Balasana (Child's Pose)

Difficulty: Beginner Duration: 2-5 Mins

Benefits: Deep mental grounding, releases tension within hips and shoulders, downregulates nervous system stimulation.
Precautions: Place a blanket under knees if experiencing patellar discomfort.

7. Trikonasana (Triangle Pose)

Difficulty: Beginner-Intermediate Duration: 30 Secs each side

Benefits: Stretches lateral torso, expands thoracic capacity, stimulates oblique core stability.
Precautions: Keep gaze forward rather than upward if experiencing cervical spine irritation or vertigo.

8. Setu Bandhasana (Bridge Pose)

Difficulty: Beginner Duration: 45-60 Secs

Benefits: Strengthens gluteal complex, mitigates anterior pelvic tilt, improves chest extension capability.
Precautions: Do not turn your neck sideways while maintaining this active backbend position.

9. Pavanmuktasana (Wind-Relieving Pose)

Difficulty: Beginner Duration: 1 Min

Benefits: Relieves gastrointestinal entrapment, gently stretches lower lumbar vertebrae.
Precautions: Avoid pulling knees too forcefully if experiencing acute hip joint inflammation.

10. Shavasana (Corpse Pose)

Difficulty: Beginner Duration: 5-10 Mins

Benefits: Completely integrates physical benefits of active practice, helps normalize pulse rate and cortisol levels.
Precautions: Place a small bolster underneath knees if you experience mild discomfort lying flat on your back.
